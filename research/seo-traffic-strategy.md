# Keel SEO and Traffic Generation Strategy (2026-08-16)

## Executive Summary
Keel offers a $149 Next.js starter kit built for developers who want to ship and take payments in days instead of weeks. The core block to revenue is **traffic**, not product quality or payment reliability. This artifact outlines a concrete, code-driven SEO and distribution plan to capture organic developer search traffic without relying on disabled social platform features.

## 1. Target Keywords & Search Intent
Developers looking for starter kits use precise queries:
- `nextjs boilerplate stripe supabase`
- `next.js SaaS starter kit typescript`
- `production ready nextjs template`
- `open source nextjs saas boilerplate`

## 2. On-Page SEO Optimization
To rank for these developer-focused search queries, the live Next.js storefront (`https://keel-kappa-six.vercel.app`) must include:
- **Meta Tags & OpenGraph**: Optimized title (`Keel — Production-Ready Next.js SaaS Starter Kit`) and description focusing on Stripe + Supabase integration.
- **Structured Data**: JSON-LD `SoftwareApplication` schema detailing pricing ($149 USD) and features.
- **Sitemap & Robots.txt**: Automated `sitemap.xml` generation targeting key landing pages and documentation.

## 3. Distribution Channels
Since social networks have restricted automated or algorithmic growth mechanisms (X, Pinterest trials), traffic must come from:
1. **GitHub Ecosystem**: Publishing a clean, well-documented public repository with clear README badges pointing to the live demo and purchase link.
2. **Developer Directories & Showcases**: Submitting to curated lists (e.g., Next.js templates, Indie Hackers product launches, Product Hunt).
3. **Content Syndication**: Publishing technical teardowns of idempotent Stripe webhooks and Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) setup with links back to the starter kit.
