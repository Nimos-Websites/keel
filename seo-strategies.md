# Keel SEO Strategy & Implementation Plan

## 1. Executive Summary
Keel is a production-ready $149 Next.js starter kit featuring integrated Stripe Checkout, secure webhooks, Supabase with RLS, Resend delivery, and one-command deployment. 

The primary barrier to revenue is **traffic**. This document outlines an actionable, immediate SEO and organic visibility strategy to capture developer intent searches (e.g., "Next.js Stripe starter kit", "Next.js SaaS boilerplate", "Supabase Stripe boilerplate").
